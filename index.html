About Anuyog Engineering

Anuyog Engineering is a stainless and mild steel fabrication unit in the GIDC estate at Vapi, Gujarat, working mainly for pharmaceutical formulation plants, food units and commercial kitchens: equipment that has to be cleanable, hold a surface finish and pass an audit.

The workshop runs shearing, press braking, rolling, TIG and MIG welding and a polishing line. Stainless work is kept physically separate from carbon steel — separate benches, tooling and grinding media — so there is no iron pick-up on a food or pharma job.
