About Anuyog Engineering

Anuyog Engineering is a stainless and mild steel fabrication unit in the GIDC estate at Vapi, Gujarat. We started in 2026 doing job work for the pharmaceutical formulation plants around Vapi and All Different Places, and that is still the heart of what we do: equipment that has to be cleanable, that has to hold a surface finish, and that has to pass an audit.

Over the years the range grew outward from that core. Change-room furniture and lockers because the plants needed them from someone who understood the clean boundary. Mixing tanks and jacketed vessels because customers wanted the process equipment from the same shop that built their utilities. Commercial kitchen equipment because a hotel group in Daman asked, and the fabrication is the same discipline. Today about seventy per cent of what leaves the shop is made to a customer's drawing or to a site measurement rather than to a catalogue size.

Our workshop runs shearing, press braking, rolling, TIG and MIG welding, and a full polishing line — matt buff, mirror through a partner unit. Fabricators who work on product-contact surfaces work only on stainless; we keep carbon steel tooling and grinding media physically separate so there is no iron pick-up on a food or pharma job.
