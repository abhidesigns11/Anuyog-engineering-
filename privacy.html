Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you give us through this website. It is written to be read, not to be skipped, so it is short.

What this website collects

Nothing automatically. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no tracking cookie and no third-party embed on these pages. Nothing you type is stored on this website, because there is no server behind it holding a database.

When you fill in an inquiry form, the details stay in your own browser until you press the button to send them. At that point they leave as a WhatsApp message or an email that you send from your own account — you can see the full text before it goes, and you can change or abandon it. If you never press send, we never receive anything.

What we receive when you contact us

When you do send an inquiry, we receive whatever you chose to include: usually your name, company, city, phone number, email address, and the description of what you need built. We also receive any drawing or photograph you attach.

What we use it for

Only to answer your inquiry and, if it becomes an order, to carry out that order — quoting, raising the drawing, invoicing, arranging delivery and handling any warranty claim afterwards. We may also contact you about a related inquiry you have made with us before.

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your details with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list because you asked for a quotation.

Who else sees it

Only the people who need it to do the job:

  • Our own staff in sales, design, production and accounts.
  • Transporters and couriers, who receive your delivery address and phone number.
  • Our accountant and, where the law requires, the tax authorities.
  • The messaging or email provider you chose to send through — their handling of the message is governed by their own terms, not ours.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become orders are kept for around two years, in case you come back to us and we can pick up where we left off. Records connected to an actual order — quotations, invoices, drawings, test reports — are kept for eight years, because tax and company law require it.

How we protect it

Inquiry emails and messages sit in company accounts protected by passwords and two-factor authentication, on devices that are only used by the staff who need them. Drawings and order files are held on a business cloud account with access limited by role. Paper records are kept in a locked office. We do not store payment card details anywhere, at any time, because we do not accept card payments.

No system is perfect. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you and the relevant authority without delay.

Your choices

Write to us at the email address below and you can ask us to send you a copy of what we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, delete your details where we are not required by law to keep them, or stop contacting you altogether. We will act within thirty days and we will not ask you why.

Children

This is an industrial equipment website intended for business buyers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

Links to other sites

Some pages link out to Google Maps or to WhatsApp. Once you follow such a link you are on their service and their privacy policy applies, not ours.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be flagged on the home page for a month.
