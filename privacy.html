Privacy policy

Last updated:

Written to be read, not skipped. The short version first, the detail after.

The short version

  • Browsing this site stores nothing about you: no cookies, no local storage, no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels.
  • If you press Send inquiry, the details you typed are saved in our database so we can reply. That is the only personal data we keep.
  • Only our staff can read inquiries, and only after logging in. The public side of the database can add an inquiry, never read one.
  • We never sell or share your details for marketing. Ask us and we delete them.

Inquiries live in a database hosted by Supabase, in . We do not store your IP address, device details or browsing history in that database. Drawings you send afterwards over WhatsApp or email stay in our business accounts and are not uploaded to this site.

What we do not do

  • No analytics and no page-view counting. We do not know who visited or what they looked at.
  • No cookies and no local storage on the public pages. (Only our own staff login page keeps a login session.)
  • No selling, renting or trading of your details, and no mailing list because you asked for a quotation.
  • No payment details, ever — we do not take payments on this site.

Who can see your inquiry

  • Our staff in sales, design, production and accounts — through a password-protected admin page.
  • Supabase, the database provider, which stores the data for us under its own security and privacy terms.
  • Transporters and our accountant, only if the inquiry becomes an order (delivery address and phone; invoices).
  • The tax authorities, where the law requires.
  • WhatsApp or your email provider, only if you choose to send the same details through them.

Other companies your browser talks to

To be completely open: loading these pages makes your browser contact a few outside services, which can see your IP address as any web server does.

  • Our web host serves the pages and keeps ordinary server logs.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr (a public code delivery network) supplies two small scripts: the smooth-scroll library and the database connector.
  • Supabase is contacted to load the product catalogue and, only when you send an inquiry, to save it.
  • Links to Google Maps and WhatsApp only connect when you click them; after that their policies apply, not ours.

How we protect it

The database refuses to let the public read inquiries — it only accepts new ones, with size limits on every field. Staff access needs an individual login. Access to the admin page is limited to accounts we have explicitly approved. No system is perfect: if we ever learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you and the relevant authority without delay.

Your choices

Write to and you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it (where the law does not oblige us to keep it), or to stop contacting you. We act within thirty days and we will not ask why. We aim to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as it applies to us.

Children

This is an industrial equipment site for business buyers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under eighteen.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the home page for a month.
