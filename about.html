How the company started

Anuyog Engineering began in 2009 as a two-man job-work shop in the GIDC estate at Vapi. The first orders were repairs and small modifications for formulation plants nearby — a cracked hopper, a leaking jacket, a change-room bench that needed to be two feet longer than anything a supplier stocked. That work taught us the thing that still shapes the business: in a regulated plant, the standard catalogue size is almost never the size that fits, and the finish matters as much as the fabrication.

Within a few years we had moved from repair into manufacture. Customers who had trusted us with a modification started asking us to build the whole item. Change-room furniture came first, then lockers and storage, then process equipment — mixing tanks, jacketed vessels, hoppers and filling vessels. Commercial kitchen work followed when a hotel group in Daman asked whether we would take on their kitchen line, and we found the discipline was the same one we already had: cleanable surfaces, no dirt traps, hardware that survives daily wash-down.

What we make

Our range falls into six families, and each one holds several products with their own size and capacity options:

  • Stainless steel furniture — stools, chairs, work tables, table cabinets and cross-over benches for cleanrooms, laboratories and change rooms.
  • Lockers and storage — personnel lockers, mobile locker banks and full-height document cupboards.
  • Tanks and vessels — mixing tanks from 50 litres to 10,000 litres, limpet and dimple jacketed vessels, storage tanks.
  • Pharma and GMP equipment — hoppers, filling vessels, seamless scoops and buckets built to product-contact surface standards.
  • Dustbins and waste handling — pedal bins, segregation sets and collection trolleys.
  • Sanitary and utility items — soap dispensers, drain traps, hand-wash stations and pass boxes.

Roughly seven items in ten leave the shop built to a customer's drawing or to a measurement one of our fitters took on site, rather than to a catalogue dimension. If what you need is not listed, that is not a problem — it is most of our work.

Our workshop

The shop runs shearing and press braking for sheet work, plate and sheet rolling for shells and cones, TIG and MIG welding, and a full polishing line taking work to matt buff or mirror. Electropolishing is done through a partner unit in Vapi when a job calls for it.

Stainless and carbon steel are kept apart. Fabricators working on product-contact surfaces use stainless-only tooling, grinding media and work benches, because iron pick-up from a shared flap disc will show as rust spotting weeks later in a customer's plant. It is an unglamorous rule and it is the one we are strictest about.

Who we supply

Most of our customers are pharmaceutical formulation and API units in the Vapi–Sarigam–Umbergaon belt, along with food processing plants, chemical units, hotels and institutional kitchens across Gujarat, Daman and Maharashtra. We also do sub-contract fabrication for two equipment OEMs who sell under their own name — which we mention because it is a fair measure of the standard the shop works to.

How to work with us

Send an inquiry through this site, on WhatsApp, or by phone. Tell us the item, the size or capacity, the material grade if you know it, the finish, and the quantity. If you have a drawing, send it — a PDF or even a clear photograph of a hand sketch with dimensions is enough to start. We reply within one working day with either a written quotation or the two or three questions we need answered before we can price it honestly.

We do not take online payments and there is no shopping cart on this website. Prices shown against products are indicative starting prices so you can size a budget; the number that counts is the one on the quotation, and it depends on grade, finish, quantity and where it has to be delivered.
