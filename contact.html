What to have ready

An inquiry moves faster when you can tell us four things: the item and quantity, the size or capacity, the material grade and finish if your specification calls for one, and where it has to be delivered. A drawing is better than all four, and a photograph of a hand sketch with dimensions on it counts as a drawing.

Getting here

The works are in the GIDC industrial estate at Vapi, Dist. Valsad, Gujarat. Vapi railway station is roughly six kilometres away and Surat airport is about two hours by road. Goods vehicles can reach the shop door — there is no lifting constraint on the approach.
